My personal study plan

You choose when you can study and what you'll learn. NeuroFocus fits block lengths, breaks and reviews to how you actually focus.

Unlock your plan

Your plan uses your own focus data, so it needs 3 recorded study sessions (each at least 2 minutes). You have 0.

  • Each session teaches NeuroFocus how long you usually stay focused and when you focus best.
  • Also take the 2-minute "How do you study?" survey so your plan starts from your preferences.

1. When can you study?

Add each day and time window you're free. NeuroFocus never books more than about three-quarters of it, so you keep catch-up time, and it skips anything already on your schedule.

2. What will you learn?

One row per topic. If there's a test or due date, add it and the plan finishes new material early and schedules spaced self-test reviews before it.
