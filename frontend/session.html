Set up your session

Deciding in advance what you'll do when distracted makes it easier to get back on track.

How it works

  1. Calibration. You look at the screen centre, its four corners and then your desk. This teaches NeuroFocus where your screen is relative to your camera.
  2. Tracking. About 10 times a second a face-landmark model estimates head direction, eye openness and iris position.
  3. Classification. Each frame is compared with your calibration: focused, looking away, looking down, eyes closed or not detected. A state must last ~2 s (1 s for closed eyes) before it counts, so blinks and quick glances are ignored.
  4. Focus Score = percentage of session time classified as focused.
NeuroFocus measures where your head and eyes point, not what you are thinking. Looking down to take notes may be counted as "looking down". It is a study aid, not a medical or diagnostic tool.
