See how focused your study time really is.

NeuroFocus AI uses your webcam and on-device computer vision to estimate when you are looking at your work, then turns it into a transparent Focus Score, session history and a study schedule that adapts to you.

Get started How it is tested

Private by design

Face landmarks are computed in your browser. The server receives six numbers per frame (head angles, eye openness, iris position), never images or video.

Personal calibration

A 30-second calibration learns where your screen is relative to your camera, so a laptop off to the side does not look like "looking away".

Schedule that plans for you

Add classes, exams and assignments. NeuroFocus finds free time before each deadline and books study blocks, then links each block to the Focus Score you achieved.

What the Focus Score is, and what it is not

It is

  • The percentage of session time your head and eyes were classified as pointing at your screen.
  • Computed by a documented, rule-based algorithm whose accuracy is measured in a reproducible simulation experiment.
  • A study aid that helps you notice patterns (time of day, subject, distraction type).

It is not

  • A measure of learning, understanding or effort.
  • A medical device. It does not diagnose or screen for ADHD or any other condition.
  • Validated on real students yet. Current accuracy figures come from simulated "virtual students" (see Research & Method).
